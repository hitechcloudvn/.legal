# HiTechCloud Legal

Official legal documents, terms, policies, licensing conditions, and compliance documentation for HiTechCloud.

## Purpose

This repository contains the official legal documents used across HiTechCloud websites, products, and services. Developers must always use the documents from this repository to ensure legal consistency.

## Documents

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Licensing Conditions
- Acceptable Use Policy
- Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Refund Policy
- Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- Security Policy
- Cookie Policy
- Other compliance documents

## For Developers

### DO

- Always reference documents from this repository.
- Keep URLs and document titles unchanged unless approved.
- Use the latest version from the `main` branch.
- Submit a Pull Request for any proposed changes.

### DON'T

- Do not copy and modify legal documents inside application repositories.
- Do not change legal wording without approval from the legal or management team.
- Do not publish draft versions.


## Versioning

Legal documents are version controlled. Every significant update should include:

- Version number
- Effective date
- Summary of changes

## Questions

If you are unsure which document to use, contact the HiTechCloud management or legal team before deploying any changes.
