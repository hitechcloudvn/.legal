## Scope

Contributions are welcome for:

- Legal document updates
- Policy corrections and clarifications
- Documentation improvements
- Licensing and compliance updates
- Bug fixes in existing legal text

## Before You Start

Please:

1. Open an issue for major changes to legal documents.
2. Keep pull requests focused on a single legal concern.
3. Prefer small, reviewable commits.
4. Use English as the primary language for all legal documents.
5. Provide Vietnamese translations where applicable.

## Contribution Policy

The repository follows an enterprise-style contribution standard:

- Use English as the primary language for all documents.
- Provide Vietnamese translations after the English version.
- Keep legal wording precise and unambiguous.
- Do not commit confidential or privileged legal materials.
- Preserve third-party attribution and notices.
- Flag legal, licensing, or commercial-impact changes clearly in the pull request.

If a contribution introduces external legal references or copied text, document the source in the pull request.

When a change affects legal identity, trademark wording, commercial-use boundaries, public branding, or repository policy, keep the wording synchronized across:

- `README.md`
- `NOTICE`
- `LICENSE`
- `COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`
- `docs/licensing.md`
- `docs/legal-notice.md`
- `docs/legal-vi.md`

## Document Standards

Legal documents in this repository should be:

- English as the primary language
- Vietnamese translation provided after the English version
- Direct and precise
- Consistent with actual company information and legal requirements
- Updated together when related behavior or policy changes

Do not include unverifiable claims, ambiguous operational steps, or inconsistent terminology.

If you add or change legal terms, update all related documents in the same pull request.

## Pull Request Checklist

Before opening a pull request, confirm:

- [ ] The change is scoped and reviewable
- [ ] English is the primary language, Vietnamese follows
- [ ] Related documents were updated if policy changed
- [ ] Legal wording is consistent across all affected files
- [ ] No confidential or privileged information is included

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or observe conduct that violates repository standards, report it privately to the maintainers.
